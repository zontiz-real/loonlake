3D models in this folder are by Quaternius (quaternius.com), released under
CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Converted from FBX to GLB for Loon Lake. Recolored in code.

Packs used:
- Animated Men Characters (Feb 2019): man_tee, man_flannel
- Animated Women Characters (Feb 2019): woman_casual, woman_jacket
- Fish Pack Animated (Apr 2018): fish_long, fish_round
- Ship Pack (Feb 2018): rowboat
- Farm Buildings (Sept 2018): barn, well, fence
- Pirate Pack (Oct 2016): rifle
- RPG Asset Pack (May 2017): barrel
- Nature Pack Essentials vol.3 (Dec 2016): rock1-3, bush1-3, maple1-4

3D models by Kenney (kenney.nl), released under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain):
- Watercraft Kit (v2.1): boat_row (boat-row-large), boat_fish (boat-speed-d), boat_speed (boat-speed-j).
  The texture is embedded into each GLB so they load as a single file.

Fish models, all CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain), from OpenGameArt.org. They were converted to GLB, turned so the
head points forward, scaled to one unit long, and had their textures dropped so the game can recolor them per species:
- fish_shark, fish_ray, fish_clown: Animated Fish Pack by Quaternius (opengameart.org/content/animated-fish): Shark, Manta ray, Clownfish
- fish_pike: "Esox - Animated Fish" by CDmir (opengameart.org/content/esox-animated-fish)
- fish_deep: "Piranha" by JamesWhite (opengameart.org/content/piranha)
- fish_cat: "3d low poly catfish" (opengameart.org/content/3d-low-poly-catfish)

Gun models (gun_revolver, gun_draco, gun_m4a1, gun_m60, gun_minigun). These were supplied by the project owner as
"Revolver PSX", "Micro Draco", "Low-poly M4A1", "Low-poly M60 machine gun" and "Low-poly M134 minigun". The original
authors and licenses have NOT been recorded yet. Confirm each one's license (and add the author credit if it requires
attribution) before publishing the game. gun_m60 has the loose ammo boxes, belts and bipod removed; gun_draco has
its textures downscaled to 512px.
