        Controls

        WASD
        Walk. Hold Shift to run.
        Mouse
        Look. Click the game first to lock the mouse, Esc to let go.
        Left click
        Uses whatever is in your hands: cast and reel with the rod, shoot a gun (hold for full auto), throw punches, take a drug.
        Right click
        Hold to zoom. With a gun out it aims down the sights, and you can still shoot.
        Space
        Jump onto rocks, barrels and crates. Hold it to keep hopping: every hop you land into adds speed. Let go early for a short hop.
        R
        Reload your gun.
        A and D
        While reeling, pull against the fish to ease the line.
        Q
        Reel your line back in.
        E
        Talk to Moss, sit down at the shack, launch your boat at the water, or step ashore.
        1–0, -, =
        Hold that hotbar item (the scroll wheel flips through them; Ctrl + wheel moves the camera). 1 is your fists, guns fill in as you buy them, then the drugs and your bag. Walk into the water to swim.

        How the lake works

        A day passes every 12 minutes. Walleye bite best at dawn and dusk. Catfish and eelpout only come out after dark.

        Bubbles on the water mark hot spots, which bite faster and hold bigger fish. The deep middle of the lake is where sturgeon live, so a longer rod pays off.

        Every few minutes a derby starts. The heaviest fish landed before the horn wins the pot. Camp is a no-shooting zone for players.

        Fish buyer

        Moss pays full price at his stand. The buyer drives out to wherever you are and keeps 30%.

        Tap a place to set a waypoint: a marker shows on your compass and in the world. Gold rings are hot spots. The channel south runs out to the big water.

        Your boat

        Launch from any shoreline or the end of the dock. Press E near land to step ashore. The big water is past the lighthouse, south down the channel.
